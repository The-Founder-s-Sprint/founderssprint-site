ABSA × THE FOUNDER'S SPRINT

Recruitment and selection

Selected jointly, for businesses Absa wants to bank

Three ways in

  • Declined or deferred by Absa for fixable reasons, referred by RMs
  • Absa SME customers preparing to grow or borrow
  • Near-bankable businesses from the Business Club and an open call

Who qualifies

  • Trading 12 months or more, with revenue
  • A founding team of up to three
  • Registered, or registering during the programme
  • A growth plan that will need finance
  • Consent to share monthly data with Absa

How they are chosen

  • Readiness Check and application
  • Interview with two coaches
  • A joint Absa and TFS selection panel

A place carries no promise of credit. Lending decisions stay with Absa. Proposed for discussion: the mix across the three tracks, a target that at least 40% of teams are women-led, and places outside Kampala.

15

A course within a discipline, such as Pitch Craft or Unit Economics. Three in each.

49

ABSA × THE FOUNDER'S SPRINT

Three years of advisory

One hour a month, every month, for 36 months

A lead coach for each team

Matched to the team's biggest gap at graduation. Any month's hour can go to a specialist coach instead.

Every session starts with the numbers

The month's check-in is reviewed first, then one problem is worked through and the plan updated.

An annual data room refresh

Model, plan and documents brought up to date once a year, ready for the next loan or raise.

Early warning

Two missed check-ins or a cash warning brings a coach call within a week, and, with consent, a note to the RM.

Milestone tracking

What we track for every team, from month zero

  • Books kept monthly

  • Credit applied for, and outcome
  • Data room complete and current

Every year

Outcomes

  • Still trading
  • Revenue growth on the year
  • Jobs created
  • Reviewed or audited accounts
  • Coach readiness score
