Financial Modelling

Barry Wojega · How this discipline works

Unit Economics
CAC & LTV Weighing what it costs to win a customer against what they’re worth over time. Payback Period How long a customer’s revenue takes to recover the cost of acquiring them. Contribution Margin What each sale earns after variable costs — the unit-level profit.
Financial Planning
Revenue Forecasting Projecting future sales from pipeline, history and grounded assumptions. Burn Rate & Runway How fast you spend cash and how many months of runway it leaves you. Cash Flow Management Timing money in and out so the business never runs dry. Tax & Compliance Meeting URA and statutory obligations cleanly, without surprises.
Capital Architecture
Valuation Methods How a company’s worth is estimated ahead of a raise or sale. Cap Table Design Structuring ownership, option pools and dilution across shareholders. Term Sheet Analysis Reading and negotiating the terms an investor puts on the table.
