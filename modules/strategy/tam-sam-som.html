Market Sizer

How big is your market, really?

Three numbers every investor asks for — and most founders guess. Put real assumptions behind them and see your revenue ceiling.

This is the pamphlet, not the textbook. It gets you thinking; your Strategy coach helps you build the defensible version.

TAMTotal Addressable Market — everyone who could ever buy, if you had no limits.
SAMServiceable Available Market — the slice you can actually reach (your geography, your segment).
SOMServiceable Obtainable Market — what you can realistically win in the next 1–3 years.
