Your data.

Your rights, and how to use them.

The Founder's Sprint holds personal data so we can run your coaching, your data room, and your account. This page explains what you can ask of us — and how to do it. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act (2019) — and the GDPR where it applies to you — you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything wrong or out of date (you can edit most of this yourself in your dashboard).
  • Delete your account and personal data (see below).
  • Object to a particular use, or withdraw consent you've given.
  • Export a copy of your data room and profile before you go.

What We Delete — And What We Keep

When you ask us to delete your account, we anonymize your personal data across the platform: your name, email, phone, bio, and the contents of your data room are removed or irreversibly scrubbed, and your profile is closed.

A small set of records is retained in de-identified form because the law requires it — chiefly financial and transaction records (payments, deposits, invoices), which tax and accounting rules oblige us to keep, and minimal security and audit logs. These no longer identify you: amounts and dates remain, your identity does not.

We action verified deletion requests within 30 days. If a payment is mid-flight, we complete or refund it first.
