◇ Data & privacy

Your data, your rights.

The Founder's Sprint holds personal data so we can run your coaching, your data room, and your account. Here's what you can ask of us — and how to do it.

Your rights

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act (2019) — and the GDPR where it applies to you — you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong or out of date (you can edit most of this yourself in your dashboard).
  • Delete your account and personal data (see below).
  • Object to a particular use, or withdraw consent you've given.
  • Export a copy of your data room and profile before you go.

What we delete — and what we must keep

When you ask us to delete your account, we anonymize your personal data across the platform: your name, email, phone, bio, and the contents of your data room are removed or irreversibly scrubbed, and your profile is closed.

A small set of records is retained in de-identified form because the law requires it — chiefly financial and transaction records (payments, deposits, invoices), which tax and accounting rules oblige us to keep, and minimal security/audit logs. These no longer identify you: amounts and dates remain, your identity does not.

We action verified deletion requests within 30 days. If a payment is mid-flight, we complete or refund it first.
