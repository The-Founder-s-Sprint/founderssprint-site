What you get

Five coaches, five disciplines, forty-nine specialties. You can take one, take three, or take the whole thing. This is exactly what sits behind each price — and where the value is.

Build with direction.

One thing is for sale, at different scales: a two-hour deep-dive on a single specialty, with the coach who owns it, that ends with a deliverable you keep. Everything else is how many of those you buy at once.

The programme breaks into 49 specialties across the five disciplines. The atomic unit — the thing you actually book — is one specialty, taught as a focused two-hour session. A single session solves one problem. Three, chosen across disciplines, close your biggest gaps. The full cohort takes you through all of them over five weeks and hands you an investor-ready data room at the end. Start wherever you are; when you upgrade, we credit what you have already paid toward the higher tier. Nothing here is priced to trap you — it is priced so a founder can start small and grow into it.
