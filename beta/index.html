§3 The Problem

Uganda's startups aren't failing from bad ideas.

They're failing from gaps no one helps them close — in pricing, financial planning, market entry, and investor readiness. The talent exists. The structure doesn't.

70%
of Ugandan startups fail — the fourth-highest rate on the continent. Most never pressure-test a revenue forecast or pricing model with anyone who's built one.
Tech In Africa 2024
43%
of 431 failed VC-backed companies died from poor product-market fit — not bad luck. They had decks, not a defensible go-to-market strategy or channel mix.
CB Insights 2024
58%
of African startup shutdowns trace to financial mismanagement. No cash-flow projection, no unit economics, no one to flag the burn rate before it killed the company.
Startup Graveyard Africa 2024
22 mo
from last raise to shutdown — the median. Founders pitch without a tested valuation method or cap table, then burn through the capital they raised.
CB Insights 2024
27%
fail from operational breakdowns — no team architecture, no governance structure, no systems designed to outlast the founding team.
