— from the Product Development & Pricing discipline, led by Patrick Ngolobe

Plenty of Ugandan founders build a product nobody asked for, then discover the problem only after the money's gone. Others build something genuinely useful and quietly give it away — priced on a guess, or on what the competitor charges, instead of what it's worth. Both are avoidable. In a mobile-first market with little room for error, getting the product and its price right is what turns effort into a business. This page breaks down the ten specialties of that craft, and why each one earns its place in a serious founder's toolkit.

Sub-category 01 · Product-Market Fit

The most expensive product to build is the one nobody asked for.

Proving the problem is real — and that your solution is the one people choose.

43%
of startups fail from poor product-market fit — building something the market didn't need. Validate the problem before you build the solution.CB Insights, 2026
Problem Validation

Proof that the problem is real, painful, and worth paying to solve — before you write a line of code. Founders who skip this build elegant answers to questions nobody's asking. We help you find the pain that's worth a product.

Solution Testing

Putting a rough version in front of real users to see what they actually do, not what they politely say. The cheapest test beats the most confident assumption. We design experiments that tell you the truth early.

PMF Signals

Knowing the difference between polite interest and genuine pull — retention, referral, the customers who'd be upset if you disappeared. Real fit is measurable. We help you read the signals before you scale on a false one.

Sub-category 02 · Product Development

Ship the smallest thing that proves the point. Then earn the right to build more.

From MVP to roadmap to the rails your product runs on.

UGX 253.7tnmoved through Uganda's mobile money in a single year, up 31.5%. Payments aren't a feature you add later — they're the rails your MVP rides on.Bank of Uganda, FY2023/24
MVP Design

The smallest version that proves the core idea — not a stripped-down everything, but a sharp something. Founders who over-build burn months perfecting features no one wanted. We help you scope the version that earns the next decision.

Roadmapping

What to build next, and — harder — what to refuse for now. A roadmap is a sequence of bets, not a wish list. We help you order the work so each release compounds on the last instead of scattering effort.

Iteration Cycles

A rhythm of build, ship, learn, repeat — short enough that you're never far from real feedback. Speed of learning beats speed of building. We install the loop that keeps the product honest.

Payments & Mobile Money

In Uganda, getting paid means MTN and Airtel money, done right. Payments that fail or confuse cost you the sale at the last step. We help you build the collection flow your market already trusts.

Sub-category 03 · Pricing Strategy

Price is the fastest lever you have — and the one founders are most afraid to pull.

Charging for the value you create, not the cost you incurred.

19%
of startups fail from unsustainable unit economics — and underpricing is the most common cause. Price for value, not by guesswork.CB Insights, 2026
Value-Based Pricing

Pricing on the value the customer gets, not the hours you put in. Cost-plus pricing leaves most of your value on the table. We help you find what the outcome is worth to the buyer — and charge accordingly.

Competitive Pricing

Reading the market's price anchors without racing to the bottom. Being the cheapest is a position anyone can take from you. We help you price against alternatives while still defending your margin.

Price Testing

Changing the price and measuring what actually happens to demand and revenue — instead of fearing the worst. A small test removes the guesswork from the scariest number on the page. We help you test your way to the right price.
