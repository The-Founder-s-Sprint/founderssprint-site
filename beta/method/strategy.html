Build with direction.

A great idea executed by the wrong team, in the wrong market, with no moat, is just a slow way to lose money. Strategy and team building is the discipline of making sure the fundamentals hold before you scale them.

— from the Strategy & Team Building discipline, led by Moses Engwau Okudu

Most Ugandan startups don't die of bad luck. They die because two co-founders never agreed on who decides, because a competitor with deeper pockets copied the idea and moved faster, or because the business never built the systems to run without the founder in every room. None of that is a funding problem — it's a strategy-and-team problem, and it's the most controllable risk a founder has. This page breaks down the eleven specialties of that craft, and why each one earns its place in a serious founder's toolkit.

Why this matters · the survival odds

Capital is scarce — but most failures are self-inflicted.

In 2024, African startups raised about US$2.2 billion. Almost none reached Uganda — so a founder here rarely gets a second cheque to fix a first mistake.

Uganda raised roughly one thirty-third of Kenya's total, so there's rarely a rescue round when things go wrong. The failures that follow are mostly self-inflicted — the wrong team, the wrong market, no moat. Strategy and team building is how a founder stops being their own biggest risk.

65%
Fail on the team
Roughly 65% of high-potential startups that fail do so because of conflict inside the founding team — over leadership, equity and direction. Team is the biggest controllable risk.
35%
Build what nobody wants
35% of startups fail because they build something the market doesn't actually want. Real strategy and market analysis separate a business from an expensive hobby.
90%
Are Uganda's private sector
MSMEs make up 90% of Uganda's private sector and ~75% of GDP. Getting strategy and team right isn't optional — it's the backbone of the whole economy.
Sources: N. Wasserman / Harvard Business School, 2012 (co-founder conflict); CB Insights, 2021; State of Entrepreneurship in Uganda 2024 (MTIC × Mastercard Foundation).
Sub-category 01 · Competitive Strategy

Your competitor isn't trying to beat you. They're trying to make you irrelevant.

Where you win, why it's defensible, and what you'll do when the market shifts.

19%
of startups fail because they get outcompeted — usually because they never built a moat before a better-resourced rival moved in.CB Insights, 2021
Market Analysis

Who's really in the market, how big it is, and where the gap is. Founders who skip this build for a market they imagined instead of the one that exists. We ground your strategy in what's actually out there.

Positioning & Moats

Why a customer picks you, and why a competitor can't simply copy it. Without a moat, every bit of traction is borrowed. We help you find the edge — distribution, trust, cost, network — that's hard to take away.

Scenario Planning

The plan for when the assumption breaks — a price war, a new entrant, a policy shift. Founders who've thought through the downside react with a plan instead of panic. We rehearse the futures before they arrive.

Sub-category 02 · Team Architecture

A company is just a group of people who agree on what matters. Design that on purpose.

Who you hire, how they work, and the rules that keep it from breaking.

23%
of startups fail because they didn't have the right team — wrong skills, missing roles, co-founders who couldn't execute together.CB Insights, 2021
Hiring Strategy

Who you need now versus who you want later, and how to find them on a startup budget. Early hires shape the culture more than any handbook. We help you hire for the gap, not the résumé.

Culture Design

The unwritten rules that decide how people behave when no one's watching. Culture forms whether you design it or not — better to choose it deliberately. We help you name the values that actually drive decisions.

Org Structure

Who owns what, who decides, and who reports to whom — before ambiguity turns into friction. A clear structure lets a small team move fast without stepping on each other. We map roles to the work that matters.

Payroll & HR Compliance

Contracts, NSSF, PAYE, and the obligations that protect both the founder and the team. Getting people-admin right early avoids disputes and penalties later. We build the basics so growth doesn't create legal exposure.

Sub-category 03 · Operational Systems

A business that only runs when the founder is in the room isn't a business yet.

The processes, goals, and legal footing that let a company run itself.

61%
of Ugandan businesses are not formally registered — locked out of finance, contracts, and the chance to scale.State of Entrepreneurship in Uganda 2024
Process Design

Turning what's in the founder's head into steps anyone can follow. The first time you write a process down is the first time the business can run without you. We help you systematise the work that repeats.

OKRs & KPIs

The handful of numbers that tell you whether you're winning, and goals the whole team can pull toward. Without them, everyone's busy and no one's sure it matters. We pick the metrics that actually move the business.

Decision Frameworks

How choices get made when the founder isn't there — and who gets to make them. Clear decision rights stop a growing team from grinding to a halt. We install the frameworks that keep momentum without chaos.

Legal & Registration

Company registration, licences, and the legal foundation a serious business stands on. In Uganda, formalising is what unlocks finance, contracts and trust. We get the structure right so growth doesn't hit a wall.
