Privacy Policy.

Effective 1 September 2026

The Founder's Sprint is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we store it, and your rights under the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 (DPPA) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Controller

The data controller is The Founder's Sprint, based in Kampala, Uganda. For data protection enquiries, contact us at Loading….

2. What We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account details: full name, email address, phone number (East African mobile number), and country.
  • Onboarding profile: business name, sector, stage, team size, revenue status, funding history, and a description of your current challenges.
  • Payment records: mobile money transaction references, payment amounts, timestamps. We do not store your mobile money PIN or full account number — payments are processed by ioTec Pay, which handles all sensitive financial data.
  • Session content: deliverables you produce during coaching sessions (financial models, pitch decks, GTM strategies), coach notes, and action items.
  • Coach onboarding: CV, professional biography, government-issued ID (for identity verification only), and profile photograph.

Information we collect automatically

  • Authentication data: login timestamps, session tokens, IP address at login (for security monitoring).
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, session bookings, deliverable completions. This helps us improve the platform and personalise recommendations.
  • Device information: browser type, operating system, screen size. Used for optimising the mobile experience.

4. Who We Share Data With

We share your data only with the following categories of processors, each bound by data processing agreements:

  • Supabase (database & authentication): stores your account data, profile, and session history. Hosted on secure cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS). Row-Level Security ensures you can only access your own data.
  • ioTec Pay (payments): receives your phone number and payment amount to initiate mobile money transactions. ioTec is registered with the Bank of Uganda and complies with Uganda's financial services regulations.
  • Mailchimp Transactional / Mandrill (email): receives your email address and name to deliver transactional emails (confirmations, reminders, curriculum access links).
  • Google Workspace (video conferencing): receives your email address and name to create Google Meet calendar events for coaching sessions.
  • Vercel (hosting): hosts the authenticated platform. Server logs may temporarily contain IP addresses.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers. We do not transfer data to any party not listed above without your explicit consent or a legal obligation.

5. Investor Visibility

If you opt in to investor visibility from your founder dashboard, a curated subset of your profile — business name, sector, stage, deliverable completion status, and coach ratings — becomes visible to vetted investors on the platform. Your personal contact details (email, phone) are never shared directly; investor introductions go through a platform-mediated request process.

You can opt out at any time. When you opt out, your profile is removed from the investor pipeline within 24 hours.

6. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with Row-Level Security policies enforcing data isolation.
  • Sensitive documents (coach ID scans, CVs) are stored in private Supabase Storage buckets accessible only to administrators via time-limited signed URLs.
  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS. No unencrypted data transmission.
  • Authentication uses Supabase Auth with magic links (founders) or email-plus-password with mandatory password change on first login (coaches/admins).
  • The Supabase service role key (admin-level access) is stored exclusively in server-side environment variables, never exposed to browsers.
  • Daily automated database backups with 7-day retention. Weekly off-platform encrypted exports to a separate storage provider.

7. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: data retained for the duration of your account plus 12 months after deactivation, to allow for reactivation.
  • Payment records: retained for 7 years to comply with Uganda Revenue Authority requirements.
  • Coach identity documents: retained for the duration of the coaching relationship. Deleted within 30 days of account deactivation, with a logged audit trail.
  • Session recordings: retained for 90 days post-session, then deleted unless you request extended storage.
  • Server logs: IP addresses in server logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days.

8. Your Rights

Under the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, and where applicable the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements for payment records).
  • Withdraw consent for investor visibility or marketing communications at any time.
  • Data portability: request a machine-readable export of your profile and deliverables.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Office of Uganda if you believe your data rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, email Loading…. We will respond within 30 days.

9. International Transfers

Your data is processed by services hosted outside Uganda (Supabase, Vercel, Google, Mailchimp). These transfers are governed by the data processing agreements with each provider, which include Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent safeguards as required by the DPPA and GDPR.

10. Children

The platform is designed for business founders and professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us immediately and we will delete the account and associated data.

11. Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach involving your personal data, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Office of Uganda within 48 hours and affected users within 72 hours, as required by the DPPA. Notification will include the nature of the breach, the data involved, and the steps we are taking to mitigate the impact.
