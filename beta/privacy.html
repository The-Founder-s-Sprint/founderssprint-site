Privacy Policy.

Effective 1 September 2026

The Founder's Sprint is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we store it, and your rights under the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 (DPPA) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Controller

The data controller is The Founder's Sprint, based in Kampala, Uganda. For data protection enquiries, contact us at Loading….

2. Who This Policy Covers

The Founder's Sprint is a multi-sided platform. This policy explains how we handle data for every type of person who uses it:

  • Founders — entrepreneurs who create an account, book coaching, build a data room, and use our interactive tools.
  • Coaches — subject-matter experts who deliver the curriculum and assess founders.
  • Mentors — experienced operators who offer paid coffee or video sessions.
  • Investors — individuals and firms who view opted-in founder profiles.
  • Service providers — businesses listed in our directory.
  • Visitors — anyone browsing our public pages.

Section 3 covers data common to everyone; the role-specific subsections explain what we additionally collect for each group.

3. What We Collect

3.1 Everyone — collected automatically

  • Authentication data: login timestamps, session tokens, and the IP address at login (for security monitoring).
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, searches run, bookings, and completions — used to improve the platform and personalise recommendations.
  • Device information: browser type, operating system, and screen size — used to optimise the mobile experience.
  • Cookies & local storage: a small set of strictly-necessary items to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3.2 Founders

  • Account details: full name, email address, phone number (East African mobile number), and country.
  • Onboarding profile & company(ies): business name(s), sector, stage, team size, revenue status, funding history, website/LinkedIn, and a description of your current challenges. A founder account can hold multiple companies.
  • Data room: documents you upload or that coaches deposit (financial models, pitch decks, GTM strategies, governance, legal, HR and other due-diligence files), plus the investor-snapshot KPIs you choose to publish.
  • Interactive Workbench: the inputs and outputs of tools you run (e.g. TAM/SAM/SOM, pricing) when you choose to save a run to your data room.
  • Coaching record: session bookings and attendance, deliverables, coach notes, action items, ratings you give, and the structured coach assessments written about you.
  • Payment records: mobile money transaction references, amounts, and timestamps. We never store your mobile money PIN or full account number — payments are processed by ioTec Pay, which handles all sensitive financial data.
  • Visibility choices: whether you have opted your profile into investor visibility, and any storage/hosting plan you hold.

3.3 Coaches

  • Application & profile: name, contact details, professional biography, credentials, headline, disciplines covered, session fee, profile photograph, and links.
  • Verification & payout (KYC): CV, government-issued ID, Tax Identification Number (TIN), and payout details. These are used only for identity verification, tax compliance, and paying you; they are stored privately and never shown to founders or investors.
  • Activity: availability windows, sessions delivered, earnings, ratings received, and the assessments you author about the founders you coach.

3.4 Mentors

  • Public profile: name, title, company, bio, areas of expertise, session formats and per-format fees, location, and photograph.
  • Verification & payout (KYC): TIN, government-issued ID, and payout details — held privately (admin/finance only), used for tax withholding, verification, and payment, and never copied into the public mentor listing.
  • Consent record: mentors are listed publicly only after giving explicit, recorded consent.
  • Recommendations: if a founder recommends you as a prospective mentor, we hold the suggested name and contact detail and the reason given until we have reviewed it.

3.5 Investors

  • Firm & member details: firm name, logo, investment thesis, sectors, stages, ticket size, geography, accreditation status, and the names/emails of the firm's member seats.
  • Activity: which opted-in founder profiles you view and the introduction requests you make.

3.6 Service providers & directory

  • Business name, category, contact details, location, and listing tier for providers who apply to the directory.

5. Who We Share Data With

We share your data only with the following categories of processors, each bound by data processing agreements:

  • Supabase (database, authentication & storage): stores account data, profiles, the data room, and session history. Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS); Row-Level Security ensures each person can access only the data they are entitled to.
  • ioTec Pay (payments): receives your phone number and payment amount to initiate mobile money transactions. ioTec is registered with the Bank of Uganda and complies with Uganda's financial services regulations.
  • Mailchimp Transactional / Mandrill (email): receives your email address and name to deliver transactional emails.
  • Google Workspace (video conferencing): receives your email address and name to create Google Meet calendar events for coaching sessions.
  • Vercel (hosting): hosts the platform API. Server logs may temporarily contain IP addresses.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers, and our products carry no advertising. We do not transfer data to any party not listed above without your explicit consent or a legal obligation.

6. Visibility & Role-Specific Sharing

Beyond our processors, some data is shown to other people on the platform — but only within strict, role-scoped limits enforced in our database, not just in the interface:

  • Founder data room & investor visibility (opt-in): your data room and snapshot are private by default. If you opt in, a curated subset — business name, sector, stage, vetted deliverables, highlights, and coach ratings — becomes visible to approved investors. Your contact details are never shared directly; introductions go through a platform-mediated request. You can opt out at any time, and your profile leaves the investor pipeline within 24 hours.
  • Coach assessments: the structured assessment a coach writes about you is visible to you (full transparency) and, once published, to approved investors on companies you have shared. Drafts never leave the coach.
  • Ratings: ratings you give a coach or mentor are tied to a verified, completed engagement. A provider's public average is shown only once enough ratings exist; individual ratings are not attributed publicly.
  • Coach & mentor public profiles: the public bio, photo, expertise, and fees of an active coach or mentor are shown to anyone. Their private verification and payout data (ID, TIN, bank/payout) are restricted to authorised staff and never published.
  • Mentor recommendations: if you recommend someone as a mentor, that suggestion is seen only by our team, who decide whether to invite them.
  • Staff: authorised Founder's Sprint staff access data strictly on a role-scoped, need-to-know basis; privileged actions are logged in an audit trail.

7. Payments

All payments are processed by ioTec Pay over MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money. We receive a transaction reference, amount, and status — never your PIN or full mobile-money account number. Coaches and mentors are engaged as consultants, so for payouts we collect a TIN and verify identity to meet Ugandan tax-withholding obligations. Per-session revenue is split with providers; settlement records are kept for tax compliance (see Retention).

8. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with Row-Level Security policies enforcing per-role, per-owner data isolation.
  • Sensitive documents (coach/mentor ID scans, CVs, TIN) live in private storage buckets, reachable only by authorised staff via time-limited signed URLs — never by a public link.
  • Public images (profile photos) are restricted to image files and size-capped to prevent abuse.
  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS. Authentication uses Supabase Auth (magic links for founders; email-and-password with a mandatory first-login change for coaches/admins).
  • The Supabase service-role key and all other secrets exist only in server-side environment variables, never in the browser or in source control. Public-facing writes (sign-ups, bookings, testimonials, mentor recommendations) are rate-limited.
  • Daily automated database backups with 7-day retention, plus weekly off-platform encrypted exports to a separate provider.

9. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: retained for the life of your account plus 12 months after deactivation, to allow reactivation.
  • Data room & saved Workbench runs: retained while your company is active; archived to a read-only cold vault or exported and deleted on request.
  • Payment & payout records: retained for 7 years to comply with Uganda Revenue Authority requirements.
  • Coach/mentor identity & payout documents (ID, TIN): retained for the duration of the relationship; deleted within 30 days of deactivation, with a logged audit trail.
  • Mentor/coach applications & founder recommendations: retained while under review and for a reasonable period after a decision, then deleted.
  • Session recordings: retained for 90 days post-session, then deleted unless you request extended storage.
  • Server logs: IP addresses retained for a maximum of 30 days.

10. Your Rights

Under the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, and where applicable the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements for payment records).
  • Withdraw consent for investor visibility or marketing communications at any time.
  • Data portability: request a machine-readable export of your profile and deliverables.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Office of Uganda if you believe your data rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, email Loading…. We will respond within 30 days.

11. International Transfers

Your data is processed by services hosted outside Uganda (Supabase, Vercel, Google, Mailchimp). These transfers are governed by the data processing agreements with each provider, which include Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent safeguards as required by the DPPA and GDPR.

12. Children

The platform is designed for business founders and professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us immediately and we will delete the account and associated data.

13. Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach involving your personal data, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Office of Uganda within 48 hours and affected users within 72 hours, as required by the DPPA. Notification will include the nature of the breach, the data involved, and the steps we are taking to mitigate the impact.
