5. Pricing & Payment

All prices on the platform are quoted in Uganda Shillings (UGX). The current pricing tiers are published on the homepage and may be updated between cohort cycles.

Payment methods

Payments are processed through ioTec Pay, which supports MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money. When you confirm a booking, a payment prompt is sent directly to your mobile phone. You authorise payment by entering your mobile money PIN on your handset. Bank transfer is available on request for institutional and hub partner bookings.

What your payment covers

  • One-on-One Coaching (UGX 500K): one 2-hour live session with one coach, one vetted deliverable, session recording and notes within 48 hours.
  • Pick 3 Bundle (UGX 1M): three 2-hour sessions across three disciplines, three deliverables, partial founder data room.
  • Full Cohort (UGX 2.5M): five weeks of group coaching across all five disciplines, all five deliverables, complete investor-ready data room, post-programme community access.

Deposits and balance payments

Full Cohort registration requires a deposit at the time of booking. The balance must be paid before the cohort start date. Access to curriculum materials is granted only after both the deposit and balance have been confirmed by ioTec Pay. No manual override exists for this process.

6. Cancellations & Refunds

You may cancel a booked session up to 48 hours before the scheduled start time for a full refund to your mobile money account. Cancellations within 48 hours of the session are non-refundable but may be rescheduled once, subject to coach availability.

For Full Cohort registrations, the deposit is non-refundable once the cohort roster has been finalised (typically 7 days before the start date). Balance payments are refundable in full if requested before the cohort start date.

If a coach is unable to deliver a scheduled session, we will either reschedule at no cost or issue a full refund at your choice.
