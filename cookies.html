This notice explains how The Founder's Sprint uses cookies and similar technologies on founderssprint.co and its authenticated subdomains. We keep our cookie usage minimal — we do not use cookies for advertising or third-party tracking.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser when you visit a website. They serve various purposes: keeping you logged in, remembering your preferences, and helping us understand how the platform is used.

3. Essential Cookies

Authentication cookies (sb-access-token, sb-refresh-token) are strictly necessary for the platform to function. Without them, you would need to log in on every page visit. These cookies cannot be disabled while using authenticated features of the platform.

4. Analytics

We currently do not use third-party analytics cookies (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no tracking scripts). Platform usage data is collected server-side through our own database, not through browser cookies. If we introduce analytics cookies in the future, we will update this notice and request your consent before setting them.

5. Third-Party Cookies

The platform loads resources from the following third-party domains. These services may set their own cookies according to their own policies:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com): loads typefaces used on the site. Google may log font requests. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Meet (meet.google.com): used for live coaching sessions. Google's cookies apply within the Meet interface. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not control these third-party cookies. We have selected these services because they are widely used, well-documented, and essential to delivering the platform experience.

6. Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, or to be notified before a cookie is set. Note that blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging in to the platform.

Common browser cookie settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data

7. Changes to This Notice

If we add new cookie categories (particularly analytics or marketing cookies), we will update this notice and request your consent before setting non-essential cookies. The current version will always be available at this URL.
