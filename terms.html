The 10% booking deposit is non-refundable — it reserves your place and your coach's time.

If you do not pay your balance in full by the 48-hour deadline, your booking is forfeited and your place is released; the 10% deposit is not refunded.

If a coach is unable to deliver a scheduled session, we will either reschedule at no cost or issue a full refund — including the deposit — at your choice.
