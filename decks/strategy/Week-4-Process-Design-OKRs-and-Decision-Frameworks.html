Strategy & Team · Week 4Objectives, Key Results & KPIs
Three words that align a team

An Objective inspires; Key Results measure; KPIs monitor.

Objective

One qualitative, motivating goal for the quarter. Where are we trying to get?

Key Results

3–5 measurable outcomes that prove you got there. Numbers, not tasks.

KPIs

The ongoing health metrics you watch all the time — your dashboard.

The shift: from "we were really busy" to "did the number move?" — focus beats activity every quarter.

Strategy & Team · Week 4Models for decisions under pressure
Decision fatigue is a real tax

Not every decision deserves the same time and worry.

The founders who burn out aren't the ones making big bets — they're the ones agonising over small, reversible ones. A handful of mental models lets you match the effort to the stakes: move fast on what's reversible, slow down only on what isn't.

The first question

"Is this a one-way door, or a two-way door?"

Strategy & Team · Week 4The cadence that auto-corrects
Systems only work if you run the loop

A weekly loop lets the company correct itself.

One short weekly review

Same time, same metrics, same questions. Look at the numbers, name what's off, decide one fix.

Metrics with owners

Each KPI has a name beside it. When it drifts, everyone knows who acts — no founder required.

Adjust, don't micromanage

The loop surfaces problems early, so you coach the system instead of chasing every task.

Process + OKRs + a weekly cadence = a business that tells you when it needs you, instead of needing you constantly.
