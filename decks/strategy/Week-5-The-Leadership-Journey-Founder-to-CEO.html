Strategy & Team · Week 5From founder instincts to CEO habits
The same instincts that save you in Survival will suffocate you in Scale.Moses Engwau Okudu

Every founder starts by doing, selling, deciding, and being the business. Those instincts are exactly right — until they aren't. Growing into a CEO means consciously letting go of the very habits that got you here.

You don't have to make all five shifts at once. You have to name the one you're still resisting — and start.

Strategy & Team · Week 5Your 90-day founder action plan
Leave with a plan, not just inspiration

The next 90 days, in three phases.

Days 1–30
Foundation
  • Write or refine your business definition
  • Score your team across the 8 domains
  • Document your top 3 broken processes
  • Write your company OKR for the quarter
Days 31–60
Architecture
  • Make your most urgent hire / close a skill gap
  • Write your competitive positioning statement
  • Define 3 non-negotiable values + behaviours
  • Run a senior-team alignment conversation
Days 61–90
Momentum
  • Run your first OKR check-in and adjust
  • Apply a decision framework to a big call
  • Build your scenario plan (base, bull, bear)
  • Verify: can the business run 72 hours without you?
