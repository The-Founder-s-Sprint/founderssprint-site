Strategy & Team · Week 1The three parameters
Every business answers three questions

Value, customer, return — get all three sharp.

What value

The specific problem you solve or outcome you create — not the product, the result.

For whom

The exact customer who feels that problem most — named, not "everyone".

At what return

How the value converts to money: price, margin, and how often they pay.

If you can't state all three in one clean sentence, the strategy on top of it will wobble. Precision here pays compound interest.

Strategy & Team · Week 1Mapping the competitive field
Map vulnerabilities — and find the white space

Name every way your customer solves this today.

Competition isn't just the company that looks like you. It's every alternative your customer can choose instead — including the cheapest one of all: doing nothing. Map them all, and the gaps between them become your white space.

The white-space question

"Where are customers badly served by every existing option — including no option at all?"

Strategy & Team · Week 1Kampala case · narrow then wide
A Kampala case study

SafeBoda won one ride, in one city, first.

The engine

Value: a safe, trackable boda ride. Customer: Kampala commuters who feared informal stages. Return: a commission per trip.

The competition

Not other apps — the thousands of informal boda riders at every stage. The status quo was the rival.

The beachhead

One product, one city, done better than anyone — safety, training, helmets. Dominate, then expand from strength.

They didn't try to be transport for everyone. They owned a precise niche in Kampala — and earned the right to grow.

Define your business in 4 moves.

⏱ 25 min solo · 35 min group
1
Your engine
One sentence
Value · customer · return — write the single sentence.
Example"We help small Kampala retailers restock on credit, earning a fee per order."
2
Your rivals
All four types
Name a direct, indirect, substitute and non-consumption rival.
Example"Non-consumption: shops that simply run out of stock."
3
Your white space
The gap
Where is every current option weakest for your customer?
Example"No one lends small, fast, and on mobile money."
4
Your beachhead
Start here
The narrowest segment you could dominate first.
Example"Pharmacies in two Kampala divisions."

How we'll review: each founder reads their one-sentence engine aloud — and names the beachhead they'll start with.
