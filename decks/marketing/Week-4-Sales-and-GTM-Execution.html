Marketing & Branding · Week 4Sales funnel design
A funnel, not a leap

Nobody goes from stranger to customer in one step.

Reach

Strangers see you. Top of funnel — the widest, cheapest layer.

Interest

Some lean in — a click, a follow, a question.

Intent

Fewer ask the price, add to cart, book a call.

Purchase

A fraction pay. This is the number that feeds you.

Every step loses people. Don't obsess over the top — find the step with the biggest drop and fix that one.

Marketing & Branding · Week 4Conversion optimisation
Where to spend your energy

Fix the weakest step, not the loudest one.

Find the drop

Look at the funnel: which step loses the most people? That's your priority.

Form one hypothesis

"If we add mobile-money checkout, more carts will convert." Test one thing.

Remove friction

Every extra field, step, or question is a place people quit. Cut them.

Doubling the top of the funnel is expensive. Lifting a weak conversion step is often free — and compounds through every stage below it.

Marketing & Branding · Week 4B2B sales for East African markets
Sell the way the market buys

In East Africa, trust closes the deal.

Relationships first

Deals move on trust and referral. Show up in person; earn the introduction.

Longer cycles

B2B and institutional sales take months. Plan cash and follow-up for it.

Mobile money

Make payment effortless — MTN & Airtel, the way customers already pay.

Proof & references

A trusted peer who already bought is your strongest closer.

The fundamentals are universal; the execution is local. Map your process to how your specific customer actually decides and pays.

Marketing & Branding · Week 4Launch planning & Go / No-Go
Plan the launch like a mission

Build to a date — then decide Go or No-Go.

Work backwards from launch day. List the must-haves — payment works, the page converts, the offer is clear, support is ready. On launch eve, run a Go / No-Go: every critical box ticked, or you hold.

A soft launch — a small invited audience first — de-risks the real one. Find the breaks before the crowd does.

Marketing & Branding · Week 4KPIs, analytics & iteration
Monitor and adapt

What gets measured gets fixed.

Set KPIs

The few metrics tied to revenue — not vanity likes.

Track the data

Analytics, the CRM, mobile-money records. Look weekly.

Test & experiment

Headlines, prices, CTAs, follow-up timing — change one thing.

Review & adapt

Against the KPI: where are you short, where are you ahead?

Sales is a system you tune, not a switch you flip. Measure, learn, adapt — every single week.
