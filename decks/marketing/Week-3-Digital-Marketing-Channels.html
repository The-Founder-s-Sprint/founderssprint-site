Marketing & Branding · Week 3Platform selection & content
Strategy before posting

Three questions before you post anything.

Where is my customer?

Go to the one or two platforms they actually use — not all of them.

What earns engagement there?

Each platform rewards a format: Reels, threads, carousels, status updates.

Can I sustain it?

A rhythm you can keep beats a burst you abandon. Consistency compounds.

Content pillars keep you consistent

Rotate a few themes — founder insight, build-in-public, customer proof, programme info — so you always know what to post and your audience knows what to expect.

Marketing & Branding · Week 3Email marketing & automation
The one channel you own

Social is rented. Email is owned.

Algorithms decide who sees your posts. Nobody stands between you and your email list. Build it from day one — a sign-up form, a lead magnet, a reason to subscribe.

Then automate a welcome sequence: a few timed emails that introduce the problem, your method, and a clear call to act — working while you sleep.

The Founder's Sprint waitlist flow

  • Welcome — what this is.
  • The problem — why it matters.
  • The team & method — why us.
  • A free framework — give value first.
  • Launch — early access to act.

Marketing & Branding · Week 3Influencer & partnership marketing
Borrow trust you haven't built yet

Other people's audiences, earned honestly.

Micro > mega

A trusted local voice with 5,000 engaged followers beats a celebrity with a million passive ones.

Partnerships

Co-market with a complementary business — shared audience, shared cost, double the trust.

Fit over reach

The right small audience converts; the wrong big one just inflates vanity metrics.

For an early brand, one authentic endorsement your customer already trusts can outperform months of cold posting.

Marketing & Branding · Week 3Paid advertising in the EAC
Buy reach — but measure it hard

Paid ads amplify what already works.

Don't pay to learn

Find your message organically first. Then put money behind the post that already lands.

Start tiny, target tight

Facebook & Google let you spend small and narrow — test before you scale.

Track to the shilling

If you can't trace ad → click → sale, you're donating, not advertising.

Paid is a multiplier, not a foundation — multiply a working funnel and you grow; multiply a broken one and you just lose money faster.

Marketing & Branding · Week 3Measure, then double down
One number per channel

Pick a KPI, watch it, move money toward what works.

Social

Engagement rate & saves — are people reacting?

Content / SEO

Organic visits & rankings — are you getting found?

Email

Open & click-through — are they listening?

Paid

Cost per acquisition — is it cheaper than the sale?

Test small, read the data, shift budget to the channel that earns. Marketing is a loop, not a launch — measure and adapt, every week.
