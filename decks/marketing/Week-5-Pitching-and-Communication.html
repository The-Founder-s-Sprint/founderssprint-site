Marketing & Branding · Week 5Effective pitching techniques
How to pitch so people lean in

Each pitch showcases your passion.

Know your audience

Tailor to the investor's interests and concerns. Research them first.

Start with a hook

A captivating opening. Put yourself at the centre of the problem.

Tell, don't list

Weave facts, examples and anecdotes into a memorable narrative.

Address concerns

Be transparent about risks — and how you'll mitigate them.

Strong call to action

State the next step clearly. Make it easy to say yes.

Emphasise the team

Show you're the right people to execute this vision.

Show traction

Milestones and proof that validate the opportunity.

Practice, practice

Memorise it. Enthusiasm is infectious — bring energy.

Marketing & Branding · Week 5Investor communication & follow-up
Fundraising is a relationship, not a transaction

The meeting is the start, not the finish.

Follow up fast

Within 24 hours: a thank-you, the answers you promised, the deck, and a clear next step.

Send a data room

Your coach-vetted documents — model, GTM, metrics — ready and organised. Trust through preparation.

Keep them warm

Short, regular updates on traction. Investors back founders they've watched execute.

Most rounds are lost in the silence after the meeting. A founder who follows up well stands out more than one with a prettier deck.

Marketing & Branding · Week 5Self-guided worksheet · in session
Self-guided worksheet · do it now

Draft your pitch in 4 moves.

⏱ 25 min solo · 35 min group
1
Your hook
First 15 seconds
One line that makes the room lean in.
Example"Every year, Ugandans abroad lose millions to bad shipping. We fixed it."
2
Problem → solution
Two sentences
The pain, then your fix — both anyone can grasp.
Example"Sourcing trusted African products is hard. We curate and ship them."
3
Your proof
Traction line
The one number or milestone that validates you.
Example"500 paying customers, 30% month-on-month growth."
4
The ask
Clear & specific
How much, and what you'll do with it.
Example"Raising $150K to reach 5,000 customers in 12 months."

How we'll review: each founder delivers their hook and ask aloud, in under a minute — and the room scores it on one thing: did you make us believe?
