Financial Modelling · Week 3What applies, at what rate, from what threshold
Four obligations, roughly in order of how often founders miss them

Put the obligation in from month one.

Corporate Income Tax

On chargeable income for resident companies — model it as a % of pre-tax profit.

VAT

On taxable supplies once turnover crosses the registration threshold.

PAYE

Withheld from salaries across progressive monthly bands — a compliance timeline, not just an expense.

Withholding tax

On specific payments (dividends, some services) — check before you sign, not after.

Not tax or legal advice

This session builds the compliance structure into your model. A licensed tax advisor must confirm the figures before you file.

Financial Modelling · Week 3Worked example · a solar water-pump manufacturer
Retail UGX 2,400,000 · materials 1,050,000 · assembly 190,000 · fixed 18,000,000/mo

Sixteen units is the line between loss and profit.

MetricResult
Variable cost per unit (materials + assembly)UGX 1,240,000
Contribution margin per unitUGX 1,160,000
Contribution margin %48.3%
Break-even volume (fixed / CM per unit)~16 units / month

Every unit above 16 in a month is pure contribution toward profit; every unit below means the month runs at a loss. That break-even number is a far more actionable sales target than a revenue goal.

Financial Modelling · Week 3Founders use these interchangeably — they're not the same
One nets off COGS; the other nets off every cost that varies with volume

The right lens for break-even and pricing.

Gross margin

Revenue − COGS. Standard accounting measure for external reporting. Doesn’t isolate variable selling costs — can look healthy while a channel loses money.

Contribution margin

Revenue − all variable costs, including selling & delivery. More work to build, but it exposes the per-product or per-channel losses gross margin hides.

Where it breaks: fixed costs mislabeled as variable, one blended CM hiding a loss-making SKU, and list price used instead of realized price (discounts and returns pull the real figure down).

Financial Modelling · Week 3Worked example · a seed-stage agritech startup
Projected exit UGX 40B in year 6 · target return 10× · raising UGX 800M

Show it as a range, never one confident figure.

MetricResult
Terminal (exit) valueUGX 40,000,000,000
Post-money (terminal / 10×)UGX 4,000,000,000
Pre-money (post-money − investment)UGX 3,200,000,000
Implied investor ownership20%

Change the terminal value by 30% and the pre-money moves the same proportion — which is why this is a range across scenarios. Where it breaks: borrowed comparables, a reverse-engineered discount rate, and a valuation with no round size attached.
