Product & Pricing · Week 5Pricing drives the whole funnel
Pricing shapes acquisition, retention and lifetime value

Four numbers investors check before anything else.

CAC

Cost to acquire one customer.

LTV

Total margin a customer brings over their life.

Gross margin

What's left after the cost of serving them.

Payback

Months to earn back the CAC.

The rule of thumb investors use: LTV at least 3× CAC, and payback inside 12 months. Pricing is the fastest way to move both.

Product & Pricing · Week 5Your 30-day pricing action plan
Leave with a plan, not just notes

The next 30 days, in three moves.

Days 1–10
Diagnose
  • Compute your unit economics (LTV, CAC, payback)
  • Audit current prices against value
  • Run a quick WTP check on one segment
  • Score your pricing maturity
Days 11–20
Design
  • Confirm your strategy and pricing model
  • Build tiers and packaging
  • Set your price band per segment
  • Map the full pricing architecture
Days 21–30
Test & track
  • Launch one safe price experiment
  • Stand up the metrics dashboard
  • Set a monthly pricing review
  • Decide: keep, refine or scale
