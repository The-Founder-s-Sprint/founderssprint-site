Pitch Craft · Session 1Open with a hook, not your history
The opening line does the heavy lifting

The first sentence earns the right to the second.

Don't open with company history, founder bios, or a dictionary definition of the market — you lose the room before the story starts. Open with something that makes the investor need to hear the next line.

Watch-out

"Founded in 2021, our company is a platform that..." — you've already lost them.

Pitch Craft · Session 1Self-guided worksheet · in session
Self-guided worksheet · do it now

Draft your narrative in 4 moves.

⏱ 25 min solo · 35 min group
1
Your hook
Under 15 words
One opening line — the problem's size or absurdity.
Example"A third of what Ugandan farmers grow rots before it sells."
2
Your arc
Five beats
One line each: problem, why now, solution, traction, vision.
Example"We get it to market in 24 hours…"
3
The feeling
Per beat
What should each beat make the investor feel?
Example"Problem → 'this is urgent and widespread.'"
4
The killer check
Self-audit
Which narrative killer is your story most at risk of?
Example"Leading with the product — fix: open on the pain."

How we'll review: read three hooks aloud — the room votes on which earns the lean-in — then one founder tells the full five-beat arc in 90 seconds.
