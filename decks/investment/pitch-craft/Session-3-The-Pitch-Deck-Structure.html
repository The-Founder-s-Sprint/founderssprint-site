Pitch Craft · Session 3Where the decision is really made
Most slides inform; five decide

Pour your effort into the five that earn the next meeting.

Problem

One painful, specific truth — not a category overview.

Traction

Your strongest evidence; real metrics and a clear trend.

Market

Credible sizing — bottom-up beats a giant top-down TAM.

Business model

How each sale makes money; honest unit economics.

The ask

Exact amount, instrument, and the milestones it buys.

The rest

Support these five. Don't let a weak slide drown a strong one.

Design principles — and the deck-killers.

Design principles
  • One idea per slide — the headline states the takeaway
  • Show, don't tell — charts, product shots, customer logos
  • Big, readable text — legible across a room
  • Consistent brand; clean, not cluttered
  • 10–12 slides for the room — depth in the appendix
Deck-killers
  • Tiny fonts and walls of text
  • Facts with no story to string them together
  • Vanity metrics dressed up as traction
  • Numbers that differ from your summary or model
  • No clear ask — or reading every slide aloud

Pitch Craft · Bringing it togetherSame story, three forms
A worked example · agri-logistics in Uganda

One story — hook, summary, title slide.

As a hook

"A third of what Ugandan farmers grow rots before it sells. We get it to market in 24 hours."

As a summary opener

"[Co] is a cold-chain logistics platform cutting post-harvest losses; 1,200 farmers, UGX Xm GMV, +18%/mo."

As a title-slide line

"[Co] — fresh produce, from farm to market in 24 hours." Then problem → solution → traction → ask.

Same hook, same numbers, same ask — in three forms. Consistency is what makes the story believable.

The capstone · one story, three forms

Produce all three — and pass the consistency check.

Your deliverable
  • A one-line hook + five-beat narrative arc (Session 1).
  • A one-page summary + five-line teaser email (Session 2).
  • A slide-by-slide deck outline — one headline per slide, 11 slides.
  • The consistency check — same hook, same numbers, same ask across all three.
